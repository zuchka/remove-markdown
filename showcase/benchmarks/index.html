Direct converters use defaults. The remark pipeline includes GFM and serialization. HTML pipelines keep heading case, labels, alt text, and unmarked lists. All configurations are in the source and snapshot.
Repeatable, with limits
Three isolated workers per size and pipeline, 100 ms warm-up, 15 timed batches per worker, and a seeded random job order. One workstation and one repeated synthetic document cannot represent every workload.
Nothing hidden in the score
Raw outputs, exact matches, whitespace-normalized matches, failures, timing samples, source hashes, and environment details travel with the report. Option-specific cases are reported separately for remove-markdown.
